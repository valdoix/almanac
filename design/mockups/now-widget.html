ALMANAC · design / mockups · floating Now widget

Three ways to make the Now widget worth a glance

All three show the same story at the same moment: Thursday evening in Saltmarsh, sixteen minutes before sunset, just after a reply that changed six things. Each one has the pill, the sky, what changed, the stakes, the people, the threads, the unspoken thoughts and what the Almanac fed the model. Only the layout differs. Click a pill to close or open its window.

Direction 1

Orrery

A pocket planetarium. The sky gets the top third of the window, a live arc with the sun sliding toward the rooftops, and everything else sits in tabs down the side. Closest to what's shipped today, and the easiest to build.

  • Pill: a mini sky dial, the time, weather, place and medallions with mood dots. A dot pulses when that mood just changed. One chip rotates through what's urgent (the ball, the debt, the sunset) and a red badge counts the changes.
  • Sky hero: the sun and moon on their real arcs over a harbour skyline, with rain when it's raining and a sunset countdown. Below it, a six-hour forecast.
  • Rail: Changed · Stakes · Cast · Threads · Unspoken · Backstage. It opens on Changed after every reply. The Unspoken tab is gone when inner voice is off.
  • Bond moves show the old value as a dashed ghost knob and the new one as a glowing knob, as in design/03 §7.
Direction 2

The Deck

Every part of the scene is a tarot card you deal through. Each card is its own object: a painted sky, a telegram, trading cards, a candle burning down, a corkboard with red string, sealed envelopes and a call sheet. It fits the storybook direction in design/03.

  • Pill: an admission ticket. The stub is torn off for the most urgent thing ("THE BALL · 3h 18m") and a wax seal carries the change count.
  • Deck: tap the top card, use the arrows or press ← → to deal the next one. After a reply, the deck opens on Since Last Page.
  • The genre card changes with the genre. A mystery gets the clue board. Horror would get the dread candle, a thriller the countdown, and so on.
  • You break the seal on an envelope to read the thought, as the Unspoken register does in chat.
Direction 3

Evening Gazette

The world prints a newspaper every turn. The biggest change is the headline and everything else is a column: society pages for the cast, a stop-press box for deadlines, classifieds for the Chekhov plants. One long read, no tabs.

  • Pill: the masthead plus a scrolling ticker of this turn's changes and what's urgent. Hover pauses it.
  • Headline: chosen from the changes by weight: knowledge shifts and due debts beat mood changes.
  • Overheard holds the inner voice. In register mode the thought is smudged ink until you click it. In inline mode it's printed plainly.
  • Confidential is redacted in player view and printed in narrator view.
