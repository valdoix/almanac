ALMANAC Ledger · drawer redesign

Three ways to reach thirteen pages in a narrow drawer

The drawer has 13 pages (Now, Cast, Bonds, Knowledge, Codex, Chronicle, Timeline, World, Lore, Creator, Recall, Craft and Settings) in a panel about 400px wide. One scrolling row of tabs can't hold them, so half the pages are hidden off the right edge. Each direction below groups them the same way and solves the problem differently. Every mockup is clickable.

Direction 1

Bookmarks

The Almanac as a bound book. Every page has a ribbon on the spine, so all 13 are always visible and one tap away.

  • Ribbon rail. An icon and a short label per page, in four colour-coded bundles: Scene, Story, Library, Engine.
  • Folio header. The page title and its chapter numeral, plus a live one-line summary.
  • Page turns. Previous and next at the foot, to read the book in order.
IM Fell English · Newsreader · DM Mono

Try it: tap any ribbon, or turn the page at the bottom.

Direction 2

Orrery

The drawer as a small planetarium. The live sky sits on top, and a dock of planets at the bottom holds the pages, like a phone app.

  • Sun button. The centre of the dock always jumps back to Now.
  • Four planets. People, Story, Library and Engine. Tapping one fans out its pages as moons on an orbit.
  • Sibling switcher. Under the sky, a switcher for the current group's pages (never more than three), so neighbouring pages are always one tap away.
Syne · Manrope · DM Mono

Try it: tap a planet to open its orbit, then pick a moon. The sun takes you home.

Direction 3

Tarot Deck

The drawer opens on a spread of cards, each showing a live count. Open a card and the rest of its suit stays in your hand at the bottom.

  • The spread. Now is the featured card. The other 12 are four suits of three, with badges for what needs attention (due debts, rejected ledger lines).
  • Your hand. Inside a page, the fanned cards switch between the suit's pages or go back to the deck.
  • Draw a card. Search jumps to any page by name. Press / or tap the search button.
Gloock · Nunito · DM Mono

Try it: pick a card, use the hand, or search with "Draw".

All three use the same four groups: Scene (Now, Cast, Bonds, Knowledge), Story (Chronicle, Timeline, World), Library (Codex, Lore, Creator) and Engine (Recall, Craft, Settings). The page content uses the extension's real data shapes and is re-skinned by each direction. The chosen design would keep the extension's skins (Almanac, Solar, Nocturne…) by mapping its colours onto the same tokens.
