Eleven skins, each in light and dark

The six original skins, rebuilt so no two feel alike, plus five new ones: serious, medieval, fantasy, futuristic and floral. Every skin has its own light and dark palette. Nothing here comes from Lumiverse's theme; each palette is fixed. Everything is drawn with the --alm-* tokens from styles.ts.

Building it: each skin becomes two token blocks, :root[data-alm-skin="x"][data-alm-mode="light|dark"] (open "Tokens" under any skin), plus a short list of overrides for its signature details. The mode would follow Lumiverse's light/dark setting unless the player picks one. Two new tokens appear: --alm-on-accent (text on the accent colour, since Solar's accent is yellow) and --v1…--v3 (voice colours tuned per mode so speakers stay readable in both).

The Almanac skin no longer borrows Lumiverse's colours. If that matters, "Follow Lumiverse" can stay as its own option.
